EVODEK® Development History

1996

Traditional Composite

Composite products were first introduced with better water resistance, lower maintenance requirements and the appearance of natural wood.

2009

EVODEK® Composite

Continued extrusion research combined wood fiber and polymer technology, improving product performance, stability and long-term service life.

2015

Innovation

New surface and color technologies were introduced to create richer, more natural-looking wood grain finishes for outdoor applications.

2018

Global Expansion

Product systems and project services expanded to more regions, supporting residential, commercial and public landscape applications.

2021

Surface Technology

More realistic textures, refined colors and improved weather resistance strengthened the product range for modern outdoor spaces.

2025

Sustainable Future

The product system continues to evolve toward efficient manufacturing, recyclable materials and long-life outdoor building solutions.
